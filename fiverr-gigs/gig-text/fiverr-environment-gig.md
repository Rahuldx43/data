## 4. FAQ

**Q: Which assets can you use?**
A: Yours, the Fab Marketplace, KitBash3D kits or Quixel Megascans. Tell me which you own so licences are respected.

**Q: Can you do both gameplay levels and cinematic shots?**
A: Yes. I balance the look against gameplay for game levels, and compose around the camera for cinematic scenes.

**Q: Which Unreal Engine versions do you work in?**
A: UE 5.3 to 5.7.

**Q: Do I need to send a blockout?**
A: No. Send one if you have it, or I will build a blockout from your references.

**Q: Will it run well?**
A: Yes. Optimisation (Nanite, LODs, level streaming) is part of the Premium package.

**Q: What do I receive?**
A: A packaged UE5 project or migrated level, plus rendered stills of the final scene.
