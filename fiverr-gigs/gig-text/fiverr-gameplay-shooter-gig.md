## 4. FAQ

**Q: Third person or first person?**
A: Both. Tell me your camera and I will set up the controls, aiming and weapon feel for it.

**Q: Can you build just one mechanic?**
A: Yes, the Core Mechanic package is for exactly that: one shooting, dodge, ability, pickup or checkpoint system.

**Q: Do I need to supply animations and models?**
A: Yes, send your character, weapon models and animations (or use free Fab / Mixamo assets). I connect them to the gameplay.

**Q: Is the code in Blueprints or C++?**
A: Blueprints by default. I also have C++ experience in UE5, so tell me if you need it.

**Q: Which Unreal Engine versions?**
A: UE 5.3 to 5.7.

**Q: Do you do multiplayer?**
A: As an extra. I have worked on online co-op and multiplayer projects.
