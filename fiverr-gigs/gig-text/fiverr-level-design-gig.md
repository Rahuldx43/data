## 4. FAQ

**Q: Which Unreal Engine versions do you work in?**
A: UE 5.3 to 5.7. Tell me your version before ordering so the project opens cleanly on your side.

**Q: Can you use my own assets?**
A: Yes. I work with client assets, Fab Marketplace packs and Quixel Megascans. I can also stay at greybox and you add the art.

**Q: Do you build only combat arenas?**
A: No. I do boss arenas, combat levels, third-person shooter maps, platformer levels, co-op maps, and cinematic environments. Message me with your genre.

**Q: Do you do Blueprint scripting too?**
A: Yes, the gameplay needed for the level: triggers, doors, checkpoints, spawns, encounter logic, basic AI behaviour trees.

**Q: Will the level run well?**
A: Yes. Optimisation is included in Premium: Nanite, LODs, level streaming and Data Layers where the level needs them.

**Q: What do I receive?**
A: A packaged UE5 project or migrated level, plus screenshots and short design notes. Source files are yours.

**Q: Do you sign NDAs?**
A: Yes, message me before sharing any build.
